# Rewrite drills

Use when the user's text is vague or flowery. Ask: what is it, who is it for, what do they do next?

| Vague | Question to answer | Plain |
|---|---|---|
| "Elevate your experience" | Experience of what? | "Motorcycles built for the open road" |
| "Crafted with passion" | Crafted what? | "Handmade furniture, built to order" |
| "Your journey starts here" | Journey to where? | "Book your first lesson" |
| "Seamless solutions" | For whom, doing what? | "Project tracking for small teams" |

Sentence-shape checks: subject and verb present; no more than one adjective per noun; no stacked abstractions ("innovative, scalable, transformative").
