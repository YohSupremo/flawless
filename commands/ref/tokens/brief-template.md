# Idea brief

- **Focal point:** what the eye lands on first and why (size, contrast, isolation).
- **Composition:** where text and subject sit, how they share the frame, how much space is left empty.
- **Hierarchy:** the reading order and the size jump between levels.
- **Content budget:** how many elements the hero holds, and what was left out.
- **Contrast strategy:** how legibility is protected, and how the accent is rationed (share of the frame).
- **Type character:** the personality of the type and why it suits the subject.
- **Copy tone:** length, directness, sentence shape.
- **Rhythm:** spacing between elements and sections; calm or dense.
- **Mood:** two or three plain adjectives.
- **Rule breakers:** anything that conflicts with `core/rules.md`, and that it will be left out.
