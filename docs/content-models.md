# Content by page type

Set `page_type` in `flawless.config.json`, or tell the agent. The copy rules are the same everywhere (plain words, no invented facts, one idea per section). What changes is which content a page needs and what the one job of the first screen is.

| Page type | First screen's job | Sections that usually earn a place | Do not invent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS | Say what the product does and for whom | What it does, how it works, pricing, one real example | Customer counts, logos, uptime, integrations |
| E-commerce | Show the product and its price | Product details, shipping and returns, reviews the user supplied | Reviews, stock levels, discounts, delivery dates |
| Editorial | Show the story or its headline | Article body, author, date, related pieces | Quotes, bylines, dates |
| Portfolio | Show the best work and who made it | Selected work, short about, contact | Clients, awards, years of experience |
| Dashboard | Show the most important number and what changed | Key figures, trend, table with filters | Data. Use clearly labeled sample data |
| Nonprofit | Say what the cause does and how to help | Impact the organization supplied, how to give or volunteer, contact | Statistics, beneficiary stories, financial claims |
| Documentation | Help the reader find the answer | Search, quick start, reference, examples | Version numbers, command output |

## Shared

- One primary action per page, named for what happens ("Buy the mug", "Book a visit"), never "Get started" or "Learn more"
- Any placeholder is labeled as sample content in the agent's reply, and in the page where a reader could mistake it for real
- Every number, price, hour, and address comes from the user
